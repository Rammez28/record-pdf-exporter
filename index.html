Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Short version: your CRM data never leaves your browser. We don't run servers that receive it, we don't use analytics or ads, and we never sell or share your data.

What the extension reads

When you click Export on a contact, company or deal page, the extension reads the content of that page — the record's properties and its activity timeline (for example names, email addresses, notes, emails, calls, meetings and tasks shown on screen) — and turns it into a PDF file inside your browser. It does not read pages you don't export, and it does not read any other website.

This content is processed only on your device. It is not uploaded, stored by us, or sent to any server. The only copy is the PDF you download.

What is stored on your device

The extension uses your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) for:

  • Your activity date range setting.
  • Your logo image, if you upload one (Pro).
  • Your Pro licence details, if you activate Pro: the licence key, its activation ID, and the details Polar returns for that licence (the email address it was bought with, plan name and expiry date).

This data stays in your browser. You can remove your logo or deactivate Pro at any time from the extension's settings page; uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

Support

Getting started

  • Open a contact, company or deal and select its Activities tab.
  • Optional: click the ⋮ menu next to the button to pick a date range (last 90 days, last year or custom).
  • Click Export. The PDF downloads to your Downloads folder.

Common questions

The button doesn't appear. Refresh the record page. The button sits next to the record's Actions menu; on very narrow screens it shows as an icon only.

Some activities are missing. Check the date range in the ⋮ menu, and any activity filter you have on in the timeline — the PDF includes what that view includes.

Some characters show as “?”. The PDF uses a standard font for Western European text; Cyrillic and Greek are converted to Latin letters, and some other scripts are not yet supported.

How do I activate Pro? Open the extension's settings (right-click the extension icon → Options), paste your licence key and click Activate.

How do I cancel? Use the customer portal link in your Polar receipt email (or sign in at polar.sh with the email you bought with). Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
